# Sales Performance Analysis | Full Year 2025

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end sales performance analysis for a fictional Kenyan retail and distribution company operating across multiple regions.

Using Microsoft Excel, I analyzed sales transactions from January to December 2025 to evaluate revenue, profitability, product performance, regional sales, and sales representative performance.

The project concludes with an **interactive Excel dashboard** that allows users to explore sales performance across different regions and product categories.

## Business Problem

The company needed better visibility into its sales performance to understand:

- Which products and regions generate the highest revenue.
- How sales and profitability change throughout the year.
- Which sales representatives meet their performance targets.
- How different sales channels contribute to overall revenue.
- Where opportunities exist to improve profitability and sales performance.

## Dataset Description

The project uses a synthetic dataset designed to simulate real-world retail operations.

**Period:** January–December 2025  
**Total Transactions:** 2,000  
**Completed Orders:** 1,749  
**Currency:** Kenyan Shillings (KES)

The workbook includes four primary datasets:

1. Sales Transactions
2. Products
3. Customers
4. Sales Representatives

## Key Findings

### 1. Monthly Sales Performance

- July recorded the highest net revenue at KES 11.37 million.
- January recorded the lowest net revenue at KES 7.82 million.
- July also generated the highest gross profit at KES 2.30 million.

### 2. Product Category Performance

- Electronics generated the highest revenue at KES 53.87 million, contributing approximately 49.7% of annual revenue.
- Home Appliances recorded the highest profit margin at 26.43%.
- Office Equipment generated KES 26.18 million in revenue.

### 3. Regional Sales Performance

- Nairobi contributed approximately 53.4% of total revenue.
- Rift Valley recorded the highest average order value at KES 73,441.94.
- Coast recorded the highest profit margin at 23.88%.

### 4. Sales Representative Performance

- Felix Ouma generated the highest annual sales revenue at KES 30.48 million.
- Amina Otieno achieved 268.04% of her annual sales target.
- Three out of ten sales representatives achieved or exceeded their annual targets.

### 5. Sales Channel Performance

- Retail Store generated the highest revenue at KES 51.98 million.
- Online Sales generated KES 33.18 million.
- Corporate Sales generated KES 23.20 million.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Improve sales representative performance:** Investigate the factors contributing to below-target performance and provide targeted support.
2. **Strengthen high-margin categories:** Explore opportunities to increase sales of Home Appliances while maintaining profitability.
3. **Improve regional performance:** Investigate opportunities to grow revenue in Western and Coast regions.
4. **Monitor seasonal sales patterns:** Use monthly performance trends to support inventory planning and promotional decisions.
5. **Optimize sales channels:** Evaluate the profitability and growth opportunities of each channel before allocating additional resources.
